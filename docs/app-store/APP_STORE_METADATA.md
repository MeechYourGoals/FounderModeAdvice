## Description

Founder Mode Advice turns the material founders study into operating guidance they can use.

Submit a supported public source or your own document. The app extracts source-grounded lessons, risks, action items, and advice tailored to a business profile. Save the result as an operating memo, organize it in your private library, and—where included in your plan—ask follow-up questions grounded in the source, export insights, or collaborate with invited teammates.

Features include:
- Structured lessons, risks, actions, and company-specific advice
- Public URL and private document analysis for supported formats
- Business profiles that tailor analysis to company stage and context
- Transcript-grounded follow-up Q&A on the eligible plan
- Searchable saved analyses, folders, bookmarks, and tags
- Native sharing, deep links, optional notifications, and Apple in-app subscriptions
- In-app restore, subscription management, privacy links, and account deletion

AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate and is not legal, financial, investment, tax, medical, or other professional advice. You are responsible for having rights to content you submit. Founder Mode Advice does not download or redistribute protected media.

A free tier is available. Optional auto-renewing subscription details and localized prices are shown before purchase. Features, billing period, full charge, renewal, trial terms (if applicable), cancellation, Terms, Privacy, and Restore must be supplied by the confirmed StoreKit/RevenueCat configuration.
