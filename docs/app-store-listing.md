## Full description

Founder Mode Advice turns founder, operator, and business-building videos into structured guidance tailored to your business — your industry, your stage, your customers, and your next decision.

Paste any public YouTube video — a founder interview, an operator deep-dive, a podcast, a conference talk — and get back lessons, risks, and action items mapped to your business. Save businesses, build folders by workstream, and open a transcript-grounded chat to ask follow-up questions directly against the video.

Features
- Personalized insight extraction from any public YouTube video
- Multiple saved business profiles with their own folders
- Ask-the-video chat grounded in the transcript
- Bookmarks and tags for your library
- Free tier with full insights view — no credit card required
- Available on iOS, Android, and web

Founder Mode Advice analyzes public content only. It is independent and does not provide private access, endorsement, or investment advice from any person or firm referenced in a video.
